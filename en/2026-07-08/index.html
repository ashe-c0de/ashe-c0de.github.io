focus on myself

2026-07-08

I don't care who is doing better than me, because the truth is — I'm not in competition with anyone else.

The only person I need to be better than is the person I was yesterday.

Last year I was different. I've grown. I've improved. I've learned a lot.

That's what really matters. It's me versus me.

Every day I wake up ready to push myself further, to challenge my limits, and become the best version of myself.

I don't let other people's success make me feel small. Their journey is theirs, and mine is mine.
